pain-to-product は、pain-to-product 運営者が使う社内向けの分析ツールです。SNS や動画サイトの公開投稿から、人々が困っていることの傾向を件数と要約で集計し、商品やサービスの企画に役立てます。

English

pain-to-product is an internal analysis tool operated by the pain-to-product operator. It aggregates public posts from social media and video sites into counts and short summaries of everyday problems, to help plan products and services.

We never publish individual posts, contact or profile the people who wrote them, or reply, repost or quote.
